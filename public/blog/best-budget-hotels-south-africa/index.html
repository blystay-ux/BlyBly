Best Budget Hotels in South Africa Under R1,000/night

By BLY. November 2026 7 min read

A good night's sleep in South Africa does not have to cost a fortune. In most cities you can find a clean, safe, well-run room for under R1,000 a night, if you know which brands to look at and when to book. This guide covers the budget hotels that regularly come in under R1,000 a night outside peak dates, plus the tricks that bring the price down further.

A note on prices: hotel rates change with dates, demand and room type. The picks below are hotels whose standard rooms regularly come in at or under R1,000 a night on normal dates. Peak weekends, school holidays and big events cost more. Always check the live rate for your dates on BLY.

What R1,000 a Night Gets You

  • A branded budget hotel: a compact, modern room with en-suite shower, Wi-Fi, secure parking and a 24-hour front desk. Breakfast is usually extra.
  • A guest house or B&B in smaller towns, often with breakfast included.
  • A self-catering unit at a holiday resort. Split between a family or group, this is often the best value per person.

What you usually do not get at this price: a pool with a view, room service or a location on the beachfront. For a stopover, a work trip or a city break where you are out all day, that is a fair trade.

The BLY. Verdict

Spend less on the room, more on the trip.

If you are out all day, a branded budget hotel such as Road Lodge or Town Lodge is the smartest money you will spend. Rooms are clean, consistent and safe, and the parking is secure. Travelling as a family or group? A self-catering unit split between everyone almost always works out cheaper per person than two hotel rooms.

How to Get a Room for Under R1,000

  • Go on a Sunday or midweek at leisure destinations, and on Friday and Saturday nights at business hotels in Sandton, Rosebank or Umhlanga Ridge, which often drop their rates over the weekend.
  • Avoid big event dates. A big concert, rugby test or conference in town can double rates overnight.
  • Check how many people the room sleeps. Some budget rooms take three guests, which cuts the cost per person.
  • Skip the breakfast if there is a coffee shop nearby. It is often the easiest saving on the bill.
  • Travel in the off-season. Cape Town in winter and KZN outside December and Easter cost much less.

Budget Hotels in South Africa: FAQs

Can you find a hotel in South Africa for under R1,000 a night?
Yes. Branded budget hotels such as Road Lodge regularly have standard rooms for under R1,000 a night on normal dates in Johannesburg, Pretoria, Durban, Cape Town and other cities. Peak dates and events cost more, so check live rates for your dates.

What are the cheapest hotel chains in South Africa?
Road Lodge is the best-known budget hotel brand in South Africa, with Town Lodge one step up. In KZN, Bayside Hotel Group also focuses on affordable stays.

Where is the cheapest place to stay in Cape Town?
Near the airport and in the Northern Suburbs, such as Bellville, rates are usually lower than in the City Bowl or on the Atlantic Seaboard. Visiting in winter, from May to August, also brings prices down.

When are hotels cheapest in South Africa?
Outside school holidays and the December and Easter peaks. Leisure areas are cheapest midweek, while business hotels in areas such as Sandton are often cheapest over the weekend.

Is it cheaper to self-cater in South Africa?
For families and groups, often yes. A self-catering unit split between several people usually costs less per person than separate hotel rooms, and you save on meals too.
