Best Beach Hotels in South Africa (Indian Ocean vs Atlantic)

By BLY. November 2026 8 min read

South Africa has close to 3,000 km of coastline and two very different oceans. On the west, the Atlantic is cold, clear and dramatic, with mountains running straight into the sea. On the east, the Indian Ocean is warm enough to swim in for most of the year, with subtropical beaches and big surf. Choosing a beach hotel starts with choosing your coast. Here is how they compare, and the best beach hotels on each side.

Indian Ocean vs Atlantic: The Short Version

Atlantic (Cape Town & West Coast)

Water

Cold. The Benguela current keeps the sea at roughly 12–16°C, even in summer. Bracing, not lingering.

Indian Ocean (KZN)

Water

Warm. The Agulhas current brings water of about 20–26°C to Durban and the KZN coast. You can swim for hours.

Atlantic

Best season

November to March: dry, sunny summers with long evenings. Winter is wet and windy.

Indian Ocean

Best season

Year-round. Winter (May to August) is dry and mild. Summer is hot and humid with afternoon storms.

Atlantic

The vibe

Scenery first: Table Mountain, the Twelve Apostles, sunset drinks and fynbos. Beaches for walking and sundowners.

Indian Ocean

The vibe

Beach holiday first: swimming, surfing, tidal pools, lifeguarded beaches and family resorts.

Between the two sits the southern Cape coast, from Hermanus to the Garden Route and the Eastern Cape. The water there is milder than Cape Town's, and the scenery is some of the country's best.

The BLY. Verdict

Want to swim? Indian Ocean. Want the view? Atlantic.

If your holiday is about long swims, the kids in the waves and beach days that last until sunset, book KZN: Umhlanga for a polished stay, Ballito for families, the South Coast for value. If it is about scenery, food and sundowners with Table Mountain in the background, book Camps Bay or Blouberg and keep your swims short. For something in between, Plettenberg Bay gives you mild water and Garden Route forest.

Beach Holiday Tips

  • December books out first. Umhlanga, Ballito, Camps Bay and Plett are busiest from mid-December to early January. Book months ahead.
  • KZN in winter is underrated: dry, warm days and lower rates than December.
  • The Cape Doctor, Cape Town's strong south-easterly summer wind, can blow for days. Clifton and Camps Bay are the most sheltered beaches when it blows.
  • Swim at lifeguarded beaches and look for the flags, especially on the KZN coast, where rip currents are common.

Beach Hotels in South Africa: FAQs

Is the Indian Ocean or the Atlantic warmer in South Africa?
The Indian Ocean is much warmer. The water off Durban and the KZN coast is usually about 20–26°C, while the Atlantic off Cape Town and the West Coast is usually about 12–16°C, even in summer.

Where is the best beach holiday in South Africa?
For swimming and a classic beach holiday, the KwaZulu-Natal coast: Umhlanga, Ballito and the South Coast. For scenery and dining, Cape Town's Atlantic Seaboard, especially Camps Bay. Plettenberg Bay is a good middle ground.

When is the best time for a beach holiday in Durban?
Durban is a year-round beach destination. Winter, from May to August, is dry, mild and quieter. Summer is hot and humid, and December is the busiest and most expensive time.

Can you swim in the sea in Cape Town?
Yes, but the Atlantic is cold, usually about 12–16°C. Beaches on the False Bay side, such as Muizenberg and St James, are normally a few degrees warmer than Camps Bay and Clifton.

Which beach is best for families in South Africa?
The KZN coast is the most family-friendly, with warm water, lifeguarded beaches and plenty of self-catering resorts in Ballito, Umhlanga, Margate and the South Coast.
