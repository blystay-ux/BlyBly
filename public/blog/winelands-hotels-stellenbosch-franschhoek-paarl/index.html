Best Winelands Stays: Stellenbosch, Franschhoek and Paarl

By BLY. November 2026 8 min read

Most people think of the Cape Winelands as two towns, Stellenbosch and Franschhoek. Add Paarl and the smaller towns around them, and you get a much bigger choice of places to stay: historic hotels in town, rooms on working wine farms, golf estates and quiet country guest houses. This guide covers the best Winelands stays in Stellenbosch, Franschhoek and Paarl, plus a few lesser-known towns worth a look.

Still deciding between the two big towns? Read our Stellenbosch vs Franschhoek guide first. This guide is about where to stay once you have chosen.

The BLY. Verdict

Town for the food, farm for the views, Paarl for the value.

If you want to walk to dinner and a wine bar, stay in central Stellenbosch or Franschhoek village. If you want to wake up in the vines with the mountains in front of you, book a wine farm hotel such as Asara or Clouds. And if you want the Winelands without the price tag or the crowds, base yourself in Paarl or Wellington and drive to Franschhoek for one day.

Winelands Planning Tips

  • Don't drink and drive. Use the Franschhoek Wine Tram, book a tour, or plan a farm stay so you can walk to the tasting room.
  • Harvest season runs from about January to March, when the farms are at their busiest and most fun.
  • Winter, from June to August, brings fireplaces, red wine, lower rates and green vineyards on the hills.
  • Book restaurants and tastings ahead in summer and over long weekends, especially in Franschhoek.
  • Combine the towns. Stellenbosch, Franschhoek and Paarl are all within about 30 to 40 minutes of each other.

Winelands Stays: FAQs

Which is the best town to stay in the Cape Winelands?
Stellenbosch is the best all-round base for a first visit, with the widest range of wine farms, restaurants and accommodation. Franschhoek suits food lovers and couples, and Paarl is the best value.

Is Paarl worth staying in?
Yes. Paarl is about 60 km from Cape Town on the N1, quieter than Stellenbosch or Franschhoek, and usually better value. It is close to wine farms such as KWV, Spice Route and Fairview, and an easy drive from Franschhoek.

Can I stay on a wine farm in Stellenbosch?
Yes. Several Stellenbosch wine farms have hotels or guest rooms, including Asara Wine Estate, Clouds Estate on the Helshoogte Pass and MolenVliet Vineyards.

How far are the Winelands from Cape Town?
Stellenbosch is about 50 km from Cape Town, Paarl about 60 km and Franschhoek about 75 km. All three are less than an hour and a half's drive.

When is the best time to visit the Winelands?
January to March is harvest season and the liveliest time. Spring, in September and October, is beautiful and cheaper, and winter brings lower rates and cosy fireside tastings.
