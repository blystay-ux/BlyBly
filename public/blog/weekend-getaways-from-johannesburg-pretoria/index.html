Best Weekend Getaways from Johannesburg and Pretoria

By BLY. November 2026 8 min read

One of the best things about living in Gauteng is how quickly you can leave it. Within an hour of Joburg or Pretoria you can be on a mountain trail in the Magaliesberg. Two and a half hours gets you into Big Five country, and in under four you can be watching the sun set over the Drakensberg or the Maluti Mountains. This guide sorts the best weekend getaways from Johannesburg and Pretoria by drive time, so you can match the trip to the weekend you have.

Drive times are approximate from central Johannesburg or Pretoria in normal traffic. Allow extra time on Friday afternoons and at the start of long weekends.

The BLY. Verdict

One night? Magaliesberg. Two nights? Pilanesberg or the Waterberg. Long weekend? The mountains.

For a quick reset, the Magaliesberg and Dinokeng are close enough that you lose almost no time on the road. For the classic Gauteng weekend, Pilanesberg is hard to beat, with Big Five game drives and Sun City next door. If you can give it three hours, the Waterberg feels like a much bigger trip. Save Clarens and the Drakensberg for a long weekend, when the extra drive pays off.

Weekend Getaway Tips

  • Leave before 2pm on Friday if you can. The N1 north, the N4 west and the N3 south all slow down badly on Friday afternoons.
  • Book long weekends early. Easter, Freedom Day, the June holidays and Heritage Day weekend fill the popular lodges weeks ahead.
  • Check for minimum stays. Many lodges and guest houses only take two-night bookings over weekends.
  • Pack for cold nights in winter, from May to August, especially in Clarens, Dullstroom and the Drakensberg, where it can drop below freezing.

Weekend Getaways from Joburg and Pretoria: FAQs

Where can I go for a weekend away from Johannesburg?
Popular weekend getaways from Johannesburg include the Magaliesberg and Hartbeespoort (about an hour), the Pilanesberg and Sun City (about 2.5 hours), the Waterberg (about 3 hours), and Clarens or the Drakensberg (3.5 to 4 hours).

What is the closest game reserve to Pretoria?
Dinokeng Game Reserve is the closest Big Five reserve to Pretoria, at about 40 minutes' drive. The Pilanesberg is about two hours away.

Where can I go for a romantic weekend near Johannesburg?
For a romantic weekend, try a private lodge in the Pilanesberg such as Tshukudu Bush Lodge, a luxury lodge in the Waterberg's Welgevonden Game Reserve, or a quiet stay in the Magaliesberg.

Is the Pilanesberg malaria-free?
Yes. The Pilanesberg, Dinokeng and the Waterberg reserves are all in malaria-free areas, which makes them popular for families with young children.

How far is Clarens from Johannesburg?
Clarens is roughly 3.5 hours' drive from Johannesburg, which makes it a good choice for a long weekend.
